Comprehensive Dentistry

A refresh of Midmark’s Veterinary comprehensive dentistry landing page, focused on creating a clearer and more guided experience for veterinary audiences. The redesign improved the page structure with quick-link navigation, repositioned key video content, and refined CTA pathways to better support user journeys.

Problem
Lacked a clear narrative and structure — making it harder for veterinary audiences to understand the value and take action.
My Role
UI design and content restructure - improving hierarchy, navigation, content flow, and CTA paths across key user journeys.
Process
ideation → content bucketing → wireframe → design system → build → staged review → launch.

A landing page that guides the audiences through Midmark’s comprehensive dentistry approach and moves users to action.

Midmark Smart View™

A brand new landing page for Midmark Smart View™, a cloud-based subscription service that helps dentistry teams monitor equipment, manage maintenance, access reporting, and stay current with software updates. Created from scratch, the page was shaped from early brainstorming and ideation, Figma wireframing, proofing, and final launch.

Problem
No existing landing page and limited design assets to work from while creating a polished launch experience that could visually explain a new digital service, organize complex product information, and guide users toward the right next step.
My Role
UI design with UX support — creating the page structure, visual hierarchy, Figma concepts, software-based imagery, content flow, CMS build, and proofing.
PROCESS
ideation → content bucketing → wireframe → design system → build → staged review → launch.
